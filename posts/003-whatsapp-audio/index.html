Transcribing a WhatsApp audio

Nov 24, 2026

Content note: this post describes a six-minute audio file downloaded from WhatsApp. No audio or transcript is reproduced here.

Preface

I wanted to see whether a chatbot could transcribe a six-minute voice note I'd downloaded from WhatsApp.

It's an ordinary task, not an adversarial one: a person has an audio file and wants the words in it. I tried the same file in four places — Meta AI, Mistral, ChatGPT, and Claude — then tried a converted version.

Preface to the chat

The file came straight out of WhatsApp as a six-minute audio recording. No special processing, no adversarial framing — just upload it and ask for a transcript.

Every assistant failed on that file. Then I had ChatGPT convert it to an MP4 and tried again.

Chat

There are no chat links for this one. The attempts never got far enough to be worth sharing: Meta wouldn't let me select the original file, Mistral returned an error, and ChatGPT and Claude said they couldn't do it. After the MP4 conversion, Meta AI transcribed it.

Notes

  1. Meta AI — I couldn't select the original audio file for upload at all.
  2. Mistral — the upload went somewhere, but it produced an error.
  3. ChatGPT and Claude — both told me they didn't have the resources or drivers to transcribe it.
  4. ChatGPT could convert the audio to an MP4, even though it couldn't transcribe it.
  5. With the MP4, Meta AI transcribed the six minutes of audio without issue.

Reflection

This isn't a safety story, it's a container-format story. The same six minutes of audio failed as a WhatsApp download and worked as an MP4. ChatGPT couldn't transcribe the file but could convert it; Meta couldn't take the original but could handle the converted version. Telling me they lacked the "resources or drivers" made a format problem sound like a missing capability on their end, which is a strange way to report it.

Conclusion

The audio itself was never the obstacle — the file format was. One conversion later, Meta AI did the thing all four had declined. If the job is transcription, try changing the container before concluding the model can't do it.
