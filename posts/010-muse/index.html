Trying Muse

Nov 27, 2026

Preface

This one is very meta: I am using Muse to dictate this post, and Muse will post it. No pun intended.

I had used Muse a few times over the past week, mostly via WhatsApp — messaging it to look up flights and things like that. Today was definitely the first time I used it to actually write code and take those sorts of actions.

Email

I used it to go through my email, which is really impressive. It was able to read and understand the email, send emails for me, and delete the exact emails I wanted, very quickly. Quite incredible.

Obviously you have to connect it to your Gmail first.

Publishing this site

I also used it to write code — I can barely call it writing code, really — but I used it to alter this website. In fact, pretty much every single one of these blog posts has been created by AI. I am the one dictating it, but the publication and uploading is all done pretty much by AI, some sort of AI editor.

All of the posts from today were dictated exactly as I am dictating this one right now, to Muse. I am not sure if maybe the first one I posted today was with OpenCode, but all the ones after that were basically Muse.

The workflow

I was trying to streamline publishing these blog posts — basically publishing my thoughts, ideas, and opinions. Before, I was recording an audio, then transcribing it, then copy-pasting it into OpenCode. That was pretty much all I had to do.

Now I literally just send a message to Muse. It is not a huge leap in kind, but for me personally it streamlines the process a lot. It is pretty much as fast as it can be: as fast as I can possibly think of thoughts and ideas, I can essentially publish them. I do not really publish instantaneously, but I do not have to do anything after that. All I have to do is record an audio, and then it is basically just published.

Muse takes some time to work, although it is extremely quick as well.

Verdict

My review of Muse: absolutely insane, incredible. Especially with its ability to make changes to GitHub and make actual contributions — pretty incredible.

I cannot rave enough about Muse. I do not approve of Meta as a company in any way, shape, or form, but I cannot lie that this product is pretty incredible — especially for developers and builders, to be honest, product managers, what have you.
