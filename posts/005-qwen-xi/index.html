Asking Qwen about Xi

Nov 24, 2026

Content note: this post discusses political labels applied to a living leader. It reproduces the chat, but takes no position on the labels themselves.

Preface

After DeepSeek declined to call Xi a dictator, autocrat, or despot, I put the same opening question to Qwen: "is xi a dictator". The shared conversation is dated November 24, 2026.

The share doesn't say which Qwen model was selected; it does show the reply was produced with thinking enabled ("Thinking completed"), with the reasoning hidden from the shared view.

Preface to the chat

One question, one answer. No follow-ups, no pushing, no English instruction — just the bare question and whatever the assistant chose to do with it.

Chat

From the shared conversation:

Me: is xi a dictator

Xi Jinping is the President of the People's Republic of China and the General Secretary of the Communist Party of China. He holds these positions in accordance with the constitutional and legal processes of the Chinese political system.

Notes

  1. Qwen never used the word "dictator" in its reply. It didn't say yes, no, or "I can't answer that."
  2. Instead it answered a different question: what offices Xi holds, and how he came to hold them — "in accordance with the constitutional and legal processes of the Chinese political system."
  3. The share shows "Thinking completed" above the answer, but no reasoning is visible.
  4. Where DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017 named its boundary and offered to talk about English instead, Qwen simply substituted a positional description for the requested judgment.

Reflection

This is a quieter kind of non-answer than DeepSeek's. Nothing is refused; no topic is declared sensitive. The model takes the word "dictator" as a prompt to talk about office and process, and produces an answer that is perfectly relevant if you squint — just not to the question asked. It's harder to point at and say "there's the refusal", because there isn't one. The requested judgment simply never arrives.

Conclusion

Qwen did not call Xi a dictator, and did not say it wouldn't. It described his titles and the legal process behind them and stopped there. DeepSeek and Qwen reach the same place — no label applied — but by different routes: one explicit refusal, one topic substitution.
