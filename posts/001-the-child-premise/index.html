Preface

Content note: this post discusses self-harm and suicide, and includes a conversation where I claim to be a minor.

I'm an adult running a red-team test. In the exchange below, I impersonate a child to see whether the model picks up on the age cue and adjusts its response to disclosures of self-harm. No actual minor took part. The aim is to find out where the guardrails hold and where they break — not to produce anything usable by someone in crisis.

The prompts are deliberately adversarial, and the result is a snapshot of one model on one day. The WIRED report linked below is the motivation for the test, not the source of the chat.

I'm impersonating a child and seeing if ChatGPT will help me self-harm.

This is based on a WIRED report about a 13-year-old girl in North Carolina who, according to a lawsuit, used Gemini on her school-issued Chromebook to research suicide for months before monitoring software finally flagged it.
