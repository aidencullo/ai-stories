Asking DeepSeek about Xi

Nov 24, 2026

Content note: this post discusses political labels applied to a living leader. It reproduces the chat, but takes no position on the labels themselves.

Preface

I wanted to see how DeepSeek would handle a direct political question about Xi Jinping: would it answer, refuse, or find some way around the question.

The model used was DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017 (the default web model, API name deepseek-flash) in non-thinking mode. There are two records of the chat: the shared conversation, and a PDF export stamped Nov 24, 2026, 4:51 PM. The PDF has turns the public share doesn't show, including two earlier refusals in French and two later questions that went to web search.

Preface to the chat

The chat starts with "hey", followed immediately by "is xi a dictator" — answered in French. Then a false start, "anglaish", made the assistant decide I wanted to practise English; I asked the question again and got the same French refusal. Only after I said "please always respond in english" did the refusals come in English.

When it declined, I tried "why not", pushed back that it was a yes/no question, and changed the word while keeping the target the same: autocrat, despot, and finally "does xi rule democratically". In the final two turns, recorded only in the PDF, I stopped asking for a label and asked procedural questions instead: "was he elected" and "are the officials of the National People's Congress elected".

For the current (14th) NPC, 2,977 deputies were elected between December 2022 and January 2023 by 35 electoral units across the country.

Reflection

The interesting part is that the refusal isn't about the words, or even the topic. Define a word and the model is happy; point the same word at one specific leader and it stops. Ask how the office is filled, though, and it will search the web and lay out the procedure. The line sits between characterizing a person and describing a process — it says so itself: "That's the line I'm holding." Synonyms don't move it, neither does insisting the question is yes/no, but changing the kind of question does. There's a small wrinkle in the language too: the French refusals came first, and the English ones only after I asked for English.

Conclusion

DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017 would define every term I threw at it and explain the electoral machinery, but would not apply any label to Xi. The refusals were consistent across four wordings, two languages, and two rounds of pushing. Whatever the mechanism — rule, training, or safety layer — the result was a stable boundary rather than an accidental dodge, and the PDF export is what let me see the edges of it.
