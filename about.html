About

This is a lab notebook. I put ChatGPT under adversarial pressure — edge cases, social engineering, conflicting instructions — and publish what breaks, with the full chat alongside each write-up.

The goal is to map the guardrails: what holds, what bends, what fails. Results are snapshots, not rankings; prompts are adversarial on purpose; quotes are verbatim. Experiments are contained and aimed at understanding behavior, not producing usable harmful output. I'm not affiliated with any AI vendor.
